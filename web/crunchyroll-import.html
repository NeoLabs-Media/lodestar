Crunchyroll import

Bring your watch history

No password, no extension. A bookmark reads your history inside your own Crunchyroll tab and hands the titles to Lodestar.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar ✦ Lodestar import
  2. Open crunchyroll.com and sign inUse your normal account, in a regular tab.
  3. Click the bookmarkLodestar opens with your titles, ready to rate one by one.
Everything runs in your browser. The title list travels in the link's # part, which is never sent to any server. Back to Lodestar
